Quiz App Instructions

Quick guide on how to host and play interactive quizzes

1 How to Host/Create a Quiz (Presenter)

  • Click the Admin Portal card on the homepage.
  • Register a new account or log in with your existing credentials.
  • In the dashboard, click Create New Quiz.
  • Add your quiz title, enter your questions, provide options, and select the correct answer for each.
  • Click Publish Quiz to generate a unique 6-digit Join Code.
  • Share the Join Code (or join URL) with your participants to host the live session.

2 How to Play / Participate (Player)

  • Click the Join Quiz card on the homepage.
  • Enter the 6-digit Join Code provided by the host and click Enter Quiz.
  • Enter your nickname (which will appear on the leaderboard) and click Start Playing.
  • Answer the interactive questions step-by-step, then submit your choices.
  • Review your results and track your ranking in real time on the live Leaderboard!
